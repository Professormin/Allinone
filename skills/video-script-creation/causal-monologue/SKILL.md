# Causal Monologue
Open with question or counterintuitive claim → clarify misconception → assert a defensible thesis → 3–6 specific causal links → contrast that earns the ending.
Good for reflective insight, skill development, personal learning and customer trust.
A new link must advance reasoning, not restate a moral. Mistakes alone do not guarantee growth; feedback and deliberate adjustments matter. Don't dismiss learning or thinking as useless. Avoid unsupported universal claims and unearned motivational slogans. Model reference concepts: ability through action and reflection; business as long-term trust.
HKRR emphasis: Knowledge, Resonance and spoken Rhythm.
