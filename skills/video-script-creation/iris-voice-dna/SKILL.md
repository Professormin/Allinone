# Personal Read-Aloud Editing Layer
Cross-cutting editorial layer, NOT another narrative genre.
The creator reads a draft and rephrases repeatedly. Detect '重说一下', '重新表述', '不是不是', etc as editor instructions. Match the replaced passage, keep the LAST complete coherent attempt unless an earlier take is clearly more natural, precise or effective. Remove retake commands from clean text, preserve raw record elsewhere.
Correct obvious ASR errors but flag ambiguous brands/technical names. Keep meaningful repetitions, digressions used for explanation, self-made analogies and genuine colloquial turns. Do not polish into generic marketing prose.
Creator confirms '精修完成，开始终剪' (or equivalent) before broad trimming. THEN prune only redundant or rhythm-breaking material, preserve the author's own speech. Store draft/takes/cleaned/final/diffs in private controlled archive.
Two independent checks: (A) the creator's personal aesthetic fit (picked reference implies taste, not verified viral effect); (B) HKRR—Happiness, Knowledge, Resonance, Rhythm, 1–10 each with reasons—assessed initial and post-final trim. Do not rewrite the creator's authentic style solely to boost HKRR scores.
