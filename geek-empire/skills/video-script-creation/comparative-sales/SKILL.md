# Comparative Sales
Problem → alternatives and tradeoffs → ideal use case → verified property or demonstration → buying qualification → clear call to action.
Do not invent test results, certification, material safety, price, scarcity, or measured advantages. Identify buyer fit and when an alternative is better. Demonstrations must be verifiable, not merely flashy.
Model reference concept: comparing small cooking pans and use cases.
HKRR emphasis: Knowledge, Resonance, persuasive Rhythm; enjoyable contrast where appropriate.
