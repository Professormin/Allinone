# Video Script Router
1. Scope gate: original knowledge-rich video scripts. Ordinary rewriting, translation and 二创 belong to a separate skill family.
2. Identify the desired viewer outcome: laugh, rethink, understand, do, discover, or decide to buy. Do NOT classify by subject keyword alone.
3. Select ONE primary sibling skill:
   - `dialogue-comedy`: fast two-voice conflict, literal misunderstandings and reversals.
   - `causal-monologue`: question, thesis, causality and reflective landing.
   - `deep-mechanism`: vivid case, challenged assumption, layered explanation.
   - `practical-tutorial`: show exact steps, visible process and troubleshooting.
   - `ai-tool-review`: product update, tested features and appropriate uses.
   - `comparative-sales`: buyer problem, fair comparison, credible proof, offer.
4. Draft a natural-to-speak text with accurate facts, relatable cases and meaningful turns, not an interchangeable promotional template. Keep third-party expression original, not copied.
5. **Dimension A — Creator taste and voice fit:** user's hand-selected sample is an affirmative taste signal, not proof it went viral. Learn why its hook, transition, sentence shape and ending appeal. Separate appreciation of craft from agreement with factual claims. Respect repeated creator read-aloud revisions.
6. **Dimension B — HKRR 1–10 each:** Happiness (fun/surprise/pleasure), Knowledge (accurate and useful insight), Resonance (shared real needs or experiences), Rhythm (hook, movement, pauses, finish). Give reasons and concrete improvements. Weight by genre, but still evaluate all four without forcing jokes into serious tutorials.
7. Score first draft before creator revisions. During read-aloud, use `../iris-voice-dna/SKILL.md`; correct false starts and clear transcription mistakes, but do not globally shorten until creator explicitly finishes. Score again after final trim.
8. Do not change author's intentional oral repetitions or established voice only to increase a score. HKRR is an editorial tool, NOT a measured retention or traffic forecast.
9. Track original draft, each read-aloud take, cleaned take, approved finish and reasons for changes in private creator-controlled storage when available.
10. New reference scripts: look at all existing structural families first. Expand an existing family rather than creating a one-script-one-skill taxonomy. Keep full reference originals private.
